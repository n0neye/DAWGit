# UX principles

How DAWGit treats the people using it. When a design choice isn't covered
by a design note, these decide it.

- **Let newcomers try it in peace.** Someone trying DAWGit out isn't told
  what could go wrong. Warnings and reminders about risks wait until they
  have settled in; before that, the app offers quietly and doesn't insist.
- **Quiet by default.** State shows as small, calm signs. Colour, banners
  and dialogs are for what needs the user now.
- **Nothing happens to your work behind your back.** DAWGit may upload in
  the background. A teammate's change comes into your files only when you
  ask for it, and every change it makes is explained.
- **Never lose a file.** When unsure, stop and say why rather than guess.
- **Don't wait for what isn't needed.** Show what is on this computer at
  once and fill in the rest. When the team can't be reached, keep working
  with what is known, and hold back only what needs the team.
- **Plain words.** Say what happened and what to do next, in the user's
  language, without version-control jargon.
